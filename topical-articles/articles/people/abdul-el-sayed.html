Abdul El-Sayed is Not a Muslim

In the November 2026 Midterm elections, Michigan elected Abdul El-Sayed as Senator. He was touted as the first Muslim-American to be elected to the US Senate.

However, this is badly mistaken. Of course, Abdul El-Sayed is not a Muslim. The standard here is simple - if Muhammad would have rejected Abdul El-Sayed as a follower, then he is certainly not a member of Muhammad's religion.

Abdul El-Sayed is a progressive Democrat, and his positions reflect that worldview. On his campaign website, he says that he will protect the rights of Americans, regardless of their "sexual orientation" or "gender identity". He supports the right for people to be Atheists, and have abortions:

Abdul El-Sayed - Money Out of Politics

I believe in the freedom of speech, the freedom of assembly, the freedom of and from religion, and the freedom of self-expression.

I believe that healthcare decisions should always stay between an individual and their doctor. And I believe that the government cannot and should not interfere with the right to pursue healthcare choices.

However, Muhammad's religion is against many things that define the worldview of progressive Democrats, and therefore, being a progressive Democrat is incompatible with being a Muslim. If one is a progressive Democrat, they are not a Muslim, and vice versa.

Consider the following:

  • Progressive Democrats believe in women's rights, and gender equality. Muhammad believed that women were generally stupid, generally wicked, and generally Hell-bound
  • Progressive Democrats value freedom of religion. Muhammad wanted to kill every person who left his religion (Sahih al-Bukhari 3017, 6922, Sahih Muslim 1676a-d), and expel every person from the Arabian Peninsula who refused to join his religion (Sahih al-Bukhari 3053, Sahih Muslim 1767a)
  • Progressive Democrats are generally against the death penalty, and mistreatment of prisoners, and prisoners of war. Muhammad beheaded 600-900 prisoners of war from the tribe of Banu Qurayza after they surrendered, and took their wives and children as captives (Sahih al-Bukhari 3804, 4121, 6262, History of al-Tabari Volume 8, pg. 34-35, Sirat Rasul Allah, Guillaume, pg. 464)

If Abdul El-Sayed is a Muslim, he is not a progressive Democrat. Since his worldview is that of a progressive Democrat, he is certainly not a Muslim.1
